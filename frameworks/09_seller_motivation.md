# Seller motivation
Seller's own accounts: capital, recent buying, valuation yields, year end, tax position. The mandate of the seller's manager and recent deals. Order of events (e.g. remove the break, then sell). Price history (falling price, failed buyers) and why. Form a book-value hypothesis that explains the price floor.
Label all of it as hypothesis unless sourced. Output: negotiating levers.

Step 1 is identification. Who is the registered owner? Look for: the Land Registry title (proprietor name and company number), the Land Registry dataset of UK companies that own property, the brochure small print, previous sale reports in the press, planning applications naming the owner, and Companies House charges that describe this property. If the seller is not identified, say so in the summary and stop reasoning about their fund, debt or motives; list how to identify them and what each step costs.
